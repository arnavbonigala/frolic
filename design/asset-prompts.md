# Image prompts for Frolic assets

Paste the style block first in every ChatGPT chat, then one asset prompt at a time.

## Style block (paste once per chat)

You are illustrating a cheerful iOS app called Frolic, where each friend is a flower in a meadow. Style for every image: soft storybook gouache with visible brush texture, rounded friendly shapes, gentle golden-hour light, no outlines, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Palette only: pink #FF7AB0 and #E9508E, cobalt #4A6FF5 and #2C4FD6, moss #46B566 and #2E9149, marigold #FFBE2E and #F29A00, tomato #FF6440 and #E2441F, lilac #AE86FF and #8B5EF2, cream #FFF8EC, sky #66AEFF to #CFE6FF, warm ink #23180F for the darkest tones. Flowers are simple 8-petal daisies with a round center. Joyful, a little whimsical, never childish, never corporate.

## 1. App icon (1024 x 1024, square, no rounded corners, no transparency)
A single large tomato-red daisy whose round cream center holds a tiny happy face, tilted slightly and bleeding off the top edge, beside a smaller cobalt daisy with a winking face, both on short green stems rising from a soft moss-green hill. A small closed lilac bud on the hill. Sky gradient from #66AEFF at the top to #CFE6FF to warm cream #FFF3DC at the horizon. Bold and readable at 60 pixels. Make three versions: light (as described), dark (deep navy night sky #10182E, flowers glowing slightly), and a single-color grayscale version for iOS tinted mode.

## 2. Plan hero images (1600 x 1200, landscape)
One per daily plan, same style, no people's faces, no text:
- Sunsets: friends' silhouettes (tiny, from behind) sitting on a big rock above a bay at golden hour, the sun low and orange.
- Outdoors: a winding trail through tall grass and wildflowers toward a hilltop tree, mid-afternoon.
- Late-night food: a glowing noodle shop window on a dark street, steam rising, string lights.
- Games: a picnic blanket in a park with a board game, cards and snacks, seen from above.
- Free stuff: an outdoor plaza with a small free concert, string lights, a crowd seen from the back.

## 3. Empty and state illustrations (1200 x 1200, transparent background)
- Garden empty: a small patch of soil with three tiny sprouts and a watering can. For "Your garden grows with every frolic."
- Faded: a single bud gently closing in soft evening light, calm and kind, not sad. For "It faded today. Tomorrow's plan is a good one."
- It's on: a bouquet of six daisies in every palette color bursting open together, petals and a few sparkles flying.
- Solo mode: one cheerful marigold daisy alone in a meadow with empty space beside it, inviting.
- Waiting: three closed buds in a row, one starting to open.

## 4. Whop money moments (1200 x 1200, transparent background)
- Your $3 is back: a small cream envelope flying back into an open hand-drawn coin purse, with a little moss-green leaf. Warm and reassuring.
- Frolic Fund: a round glass jar with a daisy-shaped lid, half full of gold coins and a few flower petals, sitting on grass.
- A no-show added $3: a single gold coin dropping into the same jar, a soft ripple of light.

## 5. Demo friend portraits (800 x 800, square, photographic)
Four friendly fictional college students, natural light, candid smiling headshots, plain soft backgrounds in their flower color: Maya (pink background), Dev (cobalt), Jordan (moss), Priya (marigold). Shoulders up, face centered, so it crops cleanly into a circle. Clearly fictional people.

## 6. Launch screen art (1290 x 2796, portrait)
A soft meadow at the bottom third with a row of six small daisies in the palette colors swaying, the sky gradient above, lots of empty sky for the app name to sit over.
